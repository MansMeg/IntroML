\section*{Oral exam question contribution}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Oral exam question contribution}

Write one possible oral exam question of your own for this assignment together with a short model answer. Include both in your assignment report.

The question should:
\begin{itemize}
\item concern material from this assignment or the associated reading or lectures,
\item be suitable for a 2--4 minute oral answer, possibly using a whiteboard,
\item test conceptual understanding rather than only calculation or code,
\item not already be included in the oral exam question list and not be very close to an existing question (e.g. just different numbers).
\end{itemize}

\emph{Note!} You are not allowed to use large language models, such as ChatGPT, at all for this oral exam question contribution.

State clearly whether the course staff may use or adapt your question and answer in a future oral exam: \textbf{Yes} or \textbf{No}.
