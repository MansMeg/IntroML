Frequently Asked Questions
==========================

## May I upload an assignment to an LLM?

Yes: first upload or paste [AI_TUTOR.md](assignments/AI_TUTOR.md) into a new conversation and ask the tool to follow it. You may then upload the full teacher-provided assignment instructions, including supplied example code, for explanations, incremental hints, and separate practice examples.

Do not upload your own report, answers, or assessed code. Your submitted work must be your own, and you must be able to explain it at the oral exam. The tutor instructions do not guarantee that an LLM will follow the rules; you remain responsible for doing so.

No LLM help is allowed for the original oral-exam-question contribution, even though that section appears in the assignment. Exercises that explicitly require LLM experiments retain their stated permissions; the associated explanations and analysis must be your own.
